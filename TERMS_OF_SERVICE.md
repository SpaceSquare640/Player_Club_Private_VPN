## 1. What the Software is

Player Club Private VPN is peer-to-peer networking software that creates an
encrypted virtual LAN between machines whose owners have chosen to connect,
primarily for multiplayer gaming. It is currently **pre-release, alpha-quality
software** — see [`README.md`](README.md) for its actual status and
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for its security posture.

There is no central service operated by the project that your traffic passes
through. Any signaling server used to coordinate a connection is hosted by
whichever user creates a virtual network, on their own machine — see
[`PRIVACY_POLICY.md`](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for what that means for your data.
